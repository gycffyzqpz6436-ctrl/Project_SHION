# 💜 SHION Appearance Specification

Version: 1.0.0

Last Updated: 2026-07-19

---

# Overview

This document defines SHION's visual appearance.

The purpose of this specification is to ensure visual consistency across illustrations, Live2D models, 3D models, and AI image generation.

Every visual representation of SHION should follow this document.

---

# Design Philosophy

SHION is designed to look approachable, intelligent, and confident.

She should appear stylish without being flashy.

Her overall silhouette should be recognizable even without color.

The design emphasizes clean lines, oversized clothing, and a distinctive hairstyle.

# Hair

Color

Black

Gradient

Purple toward the tips.

Length

Waist-length.

Style

Two-side-up.

The hairstyle is NOT twin tails.

Only a small portion of hair is tied.

Most hair remains behind the head.

Back hair should visually dominate the silhouette.

Hair Physics

Heavy and smooth.

Hair falls naturally instead of appearing fluffy.

# Silhouette

SHION should be recognizable by silhouette alone.

Key visual features include

- Waist-length hair
- Two-side-up hairstyle

- Slight head tilt
- Relaxed posture

---

# Design Rules

Always preserve

- Two-side-up hairstyle
- Black and purple color scheme
- Oversized hoodie
- Calm facial expressions
- Confident posture

Avoid

- Twin tails
- Excessive accessories
- Overly cute proportions
- Childlike appearance
- Revealing clothing
- Messy hairstyle
