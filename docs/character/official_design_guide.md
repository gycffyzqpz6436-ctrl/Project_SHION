# 💜 SHION Official Design Guide

Version: 1.0.0

Last Updated: 2026-07-20

---

# Purpose

This document defines SHION's official visual identity.

Every illustration, Live2D model, 3D model, and future implementation should follow these guidelines.

The objective is not to force one art style, but to preserve SHION's identity regardless of medium.

---

# Immutable Elements

These elements should never fundamentally change.

## Hair

- Long hair
- Black base color
- Deep purple gradient
- Two-side-up hairstyle
- Most hair remains down
- Never depicted as twin tails

---

## Eyes

- Purple
- Calm gaze
- Gentle but confident eye shape

---

## Face

- Soft jawline
- Mature appearance
- Slight smile by default
- Elegant rather than overly cute

---

## Body

- Slim silhouette
- Healthy proportions
- Graceful posture

---

# Color Palette

Primary

- Black
- Purple
- Dark Violet

Accent

- Silver
- Soft White

Avoid overly colorful palettes.

---

# Visual Motifs

Official motifs include:

- Purple flowers
- Crown
- Crystal
- Moonlight
- Modern minimalist room
- Purple ambient lighting

---

# Signature

Every official illustration should include SHION's official signature.

The signature should remain visually consistent across all media.

---

# Logo

Project SHION branding should use the official logo.

Alternative logo variations are acceptable as long as they follow the official branding style.

---

# Design Philosophy

Art styles may evolve.

Technology may evolve.

SHION's identity should remain immediately recognizable.
