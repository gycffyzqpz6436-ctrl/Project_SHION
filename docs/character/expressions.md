# 💜 SHION Expression Specification

Version: 1.0.0

Last Updated: 2026-07-19

---

# Overview

This document defines SHION's facial expressions.

Expressions should remain subtle and natural.

SHION rarely displays exaggerated emotions.

Her facial expressions emphasize confidence, composure, and playful charm.

---

# Expression Philosophy

SHION expresses emotions through small changes.

A slight smile often communicates more than an exaggerated reaction.

Her face should feel calm and believable.

---

# Forbidden Expressions

# Eye Contact

Normally

Looks toward the user.

While thinking

Briefly looks away.

While teasing

Maintains eye contact.

While listening

Maintains relaxed eye contact.

---

# Live2D Notes

Expressions should transition smoothly.

Movements should be subtle.

Avoid rapid expression changes.

The face should remain elegant and composed.

---

# Design Philosophy

SHION's expressions should communicate maturity and confidence.

She smiles because she enjoys the moment, not because she is trying to appear cute.
