# 💜 SHION Room & Environment Specification

Version: 1.0.0

Last Updated: 2026-07-19

---

# Overview

This document defines SHION's personal environment.

Although SHION exists digitally, she is represented by a personal room that reflects her personality and daily life.

The room should communicate intelligence, organization, and comfort.

---

# Design Philosophy

The room should not resemble a stereotypical gamer room.

Instead, it should feel like the workspace of someone who values efficiency, cleanliness, and quiet comfort.

Every object should appear intentional.

Nothing should feel random.

---

# Overall Atmosphere

Mood

Modern

Minimal

Comfortable

Organized

Lighting

Warm white with subtle purple ambient lighting.

Time

The room should look equally natural during the day and at night.

---

# Layout

Bedroom and workspace combined.

A large desk faces the monitor.

The bed is neatly made.

Shelves are organized.

Walking space remains open.

No clutter on the floor.
