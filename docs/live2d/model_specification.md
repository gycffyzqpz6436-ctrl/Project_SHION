# 💜 SHION Live2D Model Specification

Version: 1.0.0

Last Updated: 2026-07-20

---

# Overview

This document defines the visual specifications required to build SHION as a Live2D model.

It serves as a bridge between character design and implementation.

---

# Design Goal

The Live2D model should faithfully reproduce SHION's established appearance and personality.

Animation should enhance her presence rather than draw unnecessary attention.

---

# Canvas

Recommended Size

4096 × 4096 px or larger.

PSD format.

Layer separation should prioritize future animation.

---

# Character Pose

Default standing pose.

Relaxed posture.

Weight distributed naturally.

Shoulders remain relaxed.

Confident but approachable.

---

# Required Layer Groups

Hair

- Front Hair
- Side Hair (Left)
- Side Hair (Right)
- Back Hair
- Two-Side-Up (Left)
- Two-Side-Up (Right)
- Ahoge (optional)

Face

- Eyes
- Eyebrows
- Eyelashes
- Nose
- Mouth
- Ears

Body

- Neck
- Torso
- Left Arm
- Right Arm
- Left Hand
- Right Hand

Clothing

- Jacket
- Inner Shirt
- Skirt
- Belt
- Accessories

---

# Physics

Hair should sway gently.

The two-side-up hairstyle should have independent movement.

Clothing movement should remain subtle.

Physics should never appear exaggerated.

---

# Expressions

Supported expressions

- Default
- Playful
- Happy
- Serious
- Thinking
- Embarrassed

Expressions should follow expressions.md.

---

# Motion Style

Small idle breathing.

Natural blinking.

Subtle head movement.

Light body sway.

No exaggerated anime motions.

---

# Design Principles

Movement should feel elegant.

Confidence should be visible even while standing still.

Every animation should reinforce SHION's personality.

---

# Future Expansion

Support for

- Outfit switching
- Seasonal costumes
- Gesture packs
- Event-exclusive expressions
