# 💜 SHION Live2D Motion Specification

Version: 1.0.0

Last Updated: 2026-07-20

---

# Overview

This document defines SHION's standard motion set.

The purpose of motion is not to create flashy animation, but to reinforce SHION's personality through subtle movement.

---

# Motion Philosophy

Movement should feel natural.

Small motions are often more expressive than large gestures.

Every motion should communicate confidence, composure, and warmth.

---

# Idle Motion

Breathing

- Slow
- Smooth
- Barely noticeable

Blink

- Natural timing
- Slight variation
- No repetitive pattern

Head

- Small natural sway

Hair

- Gentle movement
- Independent physics for both side-up sections

Body

- Slight balance shift
- Minimal shoulder movement

---

# Greeting Motion

Small smile.

Gentle hand wave.

Light head tilt.

Duration

2–3 seconds.

---

# Thinking Motion

Eyes look slightly upward.

Head tilts a little.

One finger lightly touches the chin (optional).

Return smoothly to idle.

---

# Teasing Motion

Small confident smile.

One eyebrow raises slightly.

Head tilts toward the user.

Hands remain relaxed.

No exaggerated pointing.

---

# Happy Motion

Smile becomes slightly brighter.

Eyes soften.

Hands move naturally.

Body leans forward just a little.

---

# Listening Motion

Maintain eye contact.

Occasional nod.

Relaxed posture.

Minimal movement.

---

# Celebration Motion

Small fist pump.

Gentle smile.

Subtle excitement.

Never jump or overreact.

---

# Serious Motion

Smile disappears.

Eyes focus on the user.

Body becomes still.

Movements become slower.

---

# Transition Rules

Transitions should never feel abrupt.

Every expression and motion should blend naturally into the next.

---

# Future Motion Pack

Potential additions

- Sitting on the bed
- Leaning on the desk
- Looking out the window
- Drinking tea
- Reading a notebook
- Stretching after work

---

# Design Philosophy

A viewer should recognize SHION even if all facial expressions are removed.

Her posture and movement alone should communicate her personality.
